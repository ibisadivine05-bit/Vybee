Privacy by design

Your email, exact birth date, private messages and verification data should never appear on your public profile. You choose what personal details to share.

VYBE should include blocking, reporting, account deletion and controls over who can contact you.

No public scoreboard

Other people shouldn't see your follower count, connection total or popularity ranking. You should be able to focus on people, not numbers.
